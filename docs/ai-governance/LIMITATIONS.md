# LIMITATIONS — what Interve AI is not

> Read this before trusting any number on screen.

1. **Practice tool, not a hiring instrument.** Readiness levels estimate
   preparation from one session's evidence. They are not employment
   decisions and must never be used for screening, ranking, or promotion.
2. **Scores are uncalibrated.** Dimension levels follow fixed anchors with
   cited evidence, but have not yet been calibrated against human raters.
   Treat gaps as directionally useful, not precise.
3. **Speech analytics are shallow.** WPM, fillers, durations, and STT
   confidence are measured; anything about nervousness, confidence-as-trait,
   or honesty is deliberately absent and will not be added.
4. **No visual judgment.** The camera is a self-view mirror. The system
   cannot and will not infer emotion, personality, or ability from faces.
5. **LLMs hallucinate.** Evidence quotes are copied from your transcript by
   the model — spot-check them in Replay. Rationales can be fluent and wrong.
6. **Coverage is bounded.** 11 interview types share 5 rubrics; coding runs
   JavaScript/TypeScript only; whiteboard review is snapshot-based.
7. **Your data footprint.** Cloud rows persist until you delete them in the
   Privacy Center; providers you configure retain API payloads per their
   policies; offline and keyless environments degrade gracefully with
   explicit notices.
8. **English/Chinese.** The interview experience is Chinese-led; English UI
   coverage is partial and growing incrementally.
